A full-scope, blueprint-mapped review of every discipline and condition area the Part I ABS examination can draw from, built for retrieval practice rather than passive reading.

Format
Computer-based, Pearson test centers
Scored items
350 scored + 20 unscored pre-test
Sessions
2 sessions × 185 items · 4 hours each
Item styles
Single best answer + 10–15% multiple-response
Break
Optional, up to 45 minutes between sessions
Scoring
Scaled 100–900 · 300 and above passes
Major domains
2 condition groups · 17 condition areas
Disciplines tested
12, from gross anatomy to pharmacology

Structure, item counts, and condition-area weights follow the NBEO Part I ABS content matrix and current published exam information. Eponyms appear in the non-possessive form used on the exam (Horner syndrome, Descemet membrane, Schlemm canal).
