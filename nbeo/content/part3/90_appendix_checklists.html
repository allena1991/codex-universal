AppendixPrintable scored-item checklists

These checklists reproduce the item structure of the official Anterior Segment Skills Evaluation Form and Posterior Segment Skills Evaluation Form for the August 2026 to May 2027 administration, so that a study partner can score you item by item during a timed mock station. Tick each item only if you both performed and, where the item requires it, verbalised it.

Verify against the current forms on the NBEO website before your exam date, because item numbering and wording change between administrations.

Anterior segment skills station

General item

  1. Stated they would properly disinfect ocular instruments prior to using them.

Skill 1 · Biomicroscopy (items 2 to 25)

  1. Explained the purpose of the procedure to the patient.
  2. Properly examined the entire upper and lower lids and lashes.
  3. Accurately described the upper and lower lids and lashes.
  4. Properly examined the entire upper and lower lid margins and the lower puncta.
  5. Accurately described the upper and lower lid margins and the lower puncta.
  6. Properly examined the entire inferior palpebral conjunctiva using lower lid eversion.
  7. Accurately described the inferior palpebral conjunctiva.
  8. Properly examined the entire superior palpebral conjunctiva using upper lid eversion.
  9. Accurately described the superior palpebral conjunctiva.
  10. Properly examined the entire bulbar conjunctiva and sclera.
  11. Accurately described the bulbar conjunctiva and sclera.
  12. Properly examined the entire cornea using a parallelepiped.
  13. Properly examined the entire cornea using an optic section.
  14. Accurately described the cornea (epithelium, stroma, endothelium).
  15. Properly assessed the temporal anterior chamber angle using the Van Herick technique.
  16. Accurately described the temporal anterior chamber angle findings using the Van Herick technique.
  17. Properly examined the anterior chamber for cell and flare.
  18. Accurately described the anterior chamber cell and flare.
  19. Properly examined the entire iris.
  20. Accurately described the iris.
  21. Properly performed transillumination of the entire iris.
  22. Accurately described the iris transillumination.
  23. Examined the ocular tissues in a smooth, logical sequence.
  24. Properly instructed the patient throughout the procedure.

Skill 2 · Four-mirror gonioscopy (items 26 to 41)

  1. Explained the purpose of the procedure including the drop to be used.
  2. Properly prepared the gonioscopy lens and installed anaesthetic into the appropriate eye.
  3. Provided proper instructions to the patient.
  4. Inserted and utilised the gonioscopy lens in a safe and efficient manner.
  5. Obtained and maintained a clear image of the entire inferior quadrant.
  6. Accurately identified the inferior quadrant, the most posterior visible angle structure, and the iris approach.
  7. Obtained and maintained a clear image of the entire nasal quadrant.
  8. Accurately identified the nasal quadrant and the most posterior visible angle structure.
  9. Obtained and maintained a clear image of the entire superior quadrant.
  10. Accurately identified the superior quadrant and the most posterior visible angle structure.
  11. Obtained and maintained a clear image of the entire temporal quadrant.
  12. Accurately identified the temporal quadrant and the most posterior visible angle structure.
  13. Maintained control of the gonioscopy lens at all times.
  14. Removed the gonioscopy lens in a safe and efficient manner.
  15. Assessed and accurately described corneal integrity without staining at the conclusion.
  16. Performed the procedure in a smooth and logical sequence.

Skill 3 · Goldmann applanation tonometry (items 42 to 58)

  1. Explained the purpose of the procedure including the drop to be used.
  2. Instilled the appropriate anaesthetic and fluorescein combination properly in one eye.
  3. Adjusted the biomicroscope illumination system properly.
  4. Assessed and described the cornea for staining prior to tonometry.
  5. Assessed and described the quality of the tear film prior to tonometry.
  6. Set the pressure on the probe at an appropriate level.
  7. Properly positioned the tonometer and aligned the probe.
  8. Provided proper instructions to the patient, including a fixation target.
  9. Properly applanated the cornea in a safe and efficient manner.
  10. Adjusted the biomicroscope so the fluorescein semicircular patterns are symmetrical and centred.
  11. Ensured the tonometer mires are of the appropriate width.
  12. Properly aligned the tonometer mires.
  13. Safely retracted the tonometer.
  14. Accurately stated the obtained finding with the time.
  15. Assessed and accurately described the cornea using the blue filter following tonometry.
  16. Performed the procedure in a smooth and logical sequence.
  17. Educated and explained the patient's IOP.

General item

  1. Maintained proper hygiene throughout the station.

Scoring the mock: 59 items. Record the number ticked, the elapsed time at which each skill was completed, and the item numbers missed. Items marked with a crimson box in this checklist are the ones most commonly lost; check those first when reviewing.

Patient encounter station self-audit

There is no published item list for the encounter stations, because they are scored through the note and through the SP's communication matrix. This audit maps onto the five competency domains and can be used to score a mock encounter.

Skills domain: case history

  1. Introduced self by OE TRACKER number and addressed the patient as Mr. or Ms. Lee.
  2. Obtained the full HPI: location, onset, duration, severity, frequency, modifying factors, associated symptoms, prior episodes, further description.
  3. Obtained past ocular history: conditions, duration, treatment, surgery, injury, last examination.
  4. Obtained correction history: type, age of prescription, wear pattern, compliance.
  5. Obtained medical history: conditions, duration, control, last physical examination.
  6. Obtained a complete medication list, including over-the-counter agents, supplements, and drops.
  7. Obtained allergies and the reaction.
  8. Obtained surgical history.
  9. Obtained family ocular and family medical history.
  10. Obtained social history: occupation, hobbies, driving, smoking, alcohol, eye protection.
  11. Asked at least one follow-up question after reviewing the clinical data.
  12. Completed the history within 4 minutes 30 seconds.

Clinical assessment and interpretation

  1. Identified every abnormal finding in the clinical data.
  2. Ordered specific ancillary tests, each with an indication, before submitting once.
  3. Correctly interpreted the ancillary results provided, including recognising normal results.
  4. Selected the most significant diagnosis of the visit from the diagnosis list.
  5. Selected the correct location (OD, OS, OU, or N/A).

Management and documentation

  1. Documented medical therapy with drug, concentration, frequency, duration, and instructions.
  2. Documented non-medical therapy where applicable.
  3. Documented any referral with specialist, reason, and timeframe.
  4. Documented additional testing with rule-in or rule-out indications.
  5. Documented a single return-to-clinic interval, no range and no calendar date.

Patient education

  1. Named the diagnosis and then explained it in plain language.
  2. Explained the cause or mechanism.
  3. Explained the treatment, how to use it, and its expected effect and side effects.
  4. Explained warning signs requiring earlier return.
  5. Stated when the patient should come back.
  6. Checked the patient's understanding.
  7. Documented the education in the note in patient-centred language.

Communication and professionalism

  1. Used no jargon after naming the diagnosis.
  2. Did not interrupt, and reflected information back to confirm it.
  3. Spoke to the patient rather than the screen, and signalled device use appropriately.
  4. Offered options and acknowledged the practical impact of the plan.
  5. Stated which other professionals would be involved and why.
  6. Dismissed the SP explicitly, and only after all questions and all education were complete.
  7. Began typing the note by minute 11.
Final checklist before you travel
  1. Have you downloaded and read the current candidate guide, blueprint, and both evaluation forms, and reconciled them with these checklists?
  2. Have you completed the software tutorial and watched the orientation video?
  3. Can you recite the ten verbatim sentences without thinking?
  4. Have you scored at least three full anterior and three full posterior mock stations against these forms?
  5. Can you complete a six-category history in four minutes and a complete typed plan in four minutes?
  6. Do you know where you stand and where the patient looks for all eight BIO quadrants, instantly?
  7. Can you find and name a vortex vein ampulla in every quadrant?
  8. Do you have your lab coat, two forms of identification, your OE TRACKER number, and your NBEO password?
  9. Do you know your reporting date, time, and address, and have you allowed for travel?
  10. Have you planned to arrive no earlier than fifteen minutes before your listed time, with nothing that counts as luggage?
