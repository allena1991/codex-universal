Part III · PEPS

A station-by-station and item-by-item preparation manual for the twelve-station performance examination, built directly on the published blueprint and the official skills evaluation forms.

Location
NCCTO, Edison Building, Charlotte, NC
Structure
12 stations: 10 encounters + 2 skills
Cycle
3 min observation + 15 min station + 4 min break
Session length
4.5 hours · about 7 hours on site
Extended break
24 minutes after cycle 8
Scored skills
5 skills across 2 stations
Recording
Every cycle recorded, audio and video
Scoring
Pass or fail · no scaled score reported

Structure, timing, station content, and every scored item follow the NBEO Part III PEPS Candidate Guide, Blueprint and Content Outline, and the Anterior and Posterior Segment Skills Evaluation Forms for the August 2026 to May 2027 administration. Always verify against the current documents on the NBEO website before your exam date.
