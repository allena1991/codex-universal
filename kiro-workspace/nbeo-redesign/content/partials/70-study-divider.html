Every case in the bank, grouped by clinical domain, each one followed immediately by its teaching key. This is the order you learn in.

Why domain orderCornea sits with cornea, retina with retina, the neuro-ophthalmic cases together. Competing conditions land within a few pages of each other, which is where the discriminators stick. Reading a whole domain in one sitting builds the comparison the exam actually tests.
Key on the facing pageAnswer the case, then turn one page. This book is for learning, so the feedback is immediate and deliberate.
When you want it cold insteadUse the Exam Simulator. Same 350 items in exam order, cases only, every key at the back, so a session can be sat timed with nothing visible.
Working a single weaknessThe domain index gives every case with its item-type tags. To repair treatment items, work the Tx column across domains rather than reading the book front to back.
