Part 8

Session 1

Thirty-five cases, 175 items, 210 minutes. Sit it once, cold, in one block, with confidence marked on every item.

Before you startPhone away, timer visible, no notes. Answer on the capture line under each item, and mark L, M or H for confidence. The confidence mark is what makes the review efficient.
PacingItem 50 by 60 minutes, item 100 by 120, item 150 by 180. If you are behind, stop rereading stems, not standards.
When you finishScore on the sheet overleaf, by item type as well as total. Then read the teaching keys in the order given: wrong answers first, then correct answers you were unsure of, then high-confidence misses last and hardest.
What not to doDo not read a teaching key before you have answered the whole case. Do not sit Session 2 on the same day. Do not score only the total.
