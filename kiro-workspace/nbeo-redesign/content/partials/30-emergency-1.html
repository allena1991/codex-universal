Six patterns that
cannot wait

Recognise, act, and know the specific thing that must not be done. The prohibition is examined as often as the treatment.

Chemical injurysurface
RecogniseExposure history. Do not wait for acuity or pH before acting.
Do firstIrrigate immediately and copiously. Evert lids, sweep fornices for retained particles, continue until pH is neutral and stays neutral after a pause. Then grade limbal ischaemia and epithelial loss.
NeverNeutralise alkali with acid. Delay irrigation to find equipment.
Open globetrauma
RecogniseFull-thickness wound, Seidel, peaked pupil, uveal prolapse, low IOP with a mechanism.
Do firstRigid shield, nil by mouth, antiemetic and analgesia, tetanus and systemic antibiotics per protocol, CT orbits when appropriate, immediate surgical referral.
NeverTonometry, gonioscopy, ultrasound, or any pressure on the globe.
Orbital cellulitisorbit
RecogniseLid swelling plus any of: painful or restricted motility, proptosis, reduced acuity or colour, RAPD, raised IOP, fever, severe chemosis.
Do firstEmergency department, contrast CT of orbits and sinuses, intravenous antibiotics, ophthalmology and ENT. Drain a subperiosteal abscess when vision or the apex is threatened.
NeverCall it preseptal until orbital signs have been actively excluded.
Microbial keratitiscornea
RecogniseContact lens wear, epithelial defect over a stromal infiltrate, chamber reaction, pain, central location.
Do firstMeasure and photograph. Culture central, large, deep, atypical, post-surgical, or unresponsive ulcers. Intensive topical antimicrobial, review inside 24 hours.
NeverSend a central ulcer home on conjunctivitis dosing, or patch it.
Acute angle closureglaucoma
RecognisePain, halos, nausea, corneal oedema, shallow chamber, mid-dilated fixed pupil, very high IOP.
Do firstAqueous suppressants the patient can safely take, systemic acetazolamide if appropriate, control inflammation and nausea, then definitive iridotomy. Assess the fellow eye.
NeverLead with pilocarpine at extreme pressure: an ischaemic sphincter will not respond.
Giant cell arteritissystemic
RecogniseOver 50 with new headache, jaw claudication, scalp tenderness, polymyalgia or constitutional symptoms, chalky pallid disc oedema.
Do firstESR, CRP, and platelets, and start high-dose systemic corticosteroid immediately. Temporal artery imaging or biopsy follows treatment.
NeverLet a normal ESR reassure you, or wait for biopsy. The fellow eye is what is at stake.
