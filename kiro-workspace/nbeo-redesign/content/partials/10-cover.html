What changed in this edition

The previous key was 82% option A, and 95% in Session 2: guessing A scored 272 of 333 items with no stem read. Letters are now generated to a distribution and audited on page {{PGREF:keyint}}, options are length-matched, and every rationale explains the tempting wrong answer as well as the right one.

How the two volumes divide

Each volume is complete: the same reference apparatus, then one session. Cases come first and the teaching keys follow in Part 9, so a session can be sat cold. Volume {{OTHERVOL}} carries the other {{VOLITEMS}} items, on the same letter distribution, so the two scores are comparable.
