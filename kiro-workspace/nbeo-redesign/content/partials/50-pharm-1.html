Anchor, caveat, monitor

A memorised dose used in the wrong tissue or the wrong host is a wrong answer.

Antimicrobial anchors

IndicationAnchorCaveat that gets examined
Bacterial keratitisFluoroquinolone for small peripheral ulcers; fortified therapy for central, deep or resistant disease. Load every 5-15 minutes, then hourly.Culture first when central, large, deep or unresponsive. Do not wait for results to treat.
HSV epithelial keratitisAciclovir 400 mg five times daily, or valaciclovir 500 mg three times daily, 7-10 days. Topical ganciclovir 0.15% is an alternative.No steroid over an active epithelial ulcer. Steroid belongs to immune stromal or endothelial disease, with antiviral cover.
Herpes zoster ophthalmicusValaciclovir 1 g three times daily, or aciclovir 800 mg five times daily, 7-10 days, renally adjusted.Ocular disease lags the rash. A quiet first examination does not end surveillance.
Fungal keratitisNatamycin 5% hourly initially for filamentous organisms, prolonged course, cornea specialist involved.Corticosteroid accelerates progression. Vegetative trauma and feathery margins are the clues.
Acanthamoeba keratitisIntensive biguanide therapy, PHMB or chlorhexidine 0.02%, often with a diamidine, for months.Pain out of proportion, water exposure. Early disease is mislabelled HSV.
Hyperacute conjunctivitisSystemic ceftriaxone-based treatment per current CDC guidance, saline lavage, STI testing, partner care.Copious purulence with corneal risk is an emergency, not a drop problem.

Contraindication anchors

Topical beta-blockercaution
Avoid
Bronchospastic asthma or COPD, bradycardia, heart block, decompensated heart failure.
Reduce
Punctal occlusion and lid closure lower systemic absorption.
Trap
A resting pulse in the low 50s is in the stem for a reason.
Alpha-2 agonistcaution
Avoid
Brimonidine in infants and very young children: CNS depression and apnoea.
Expect
Fatigue, dry mouth, late follicular allergy, hypotension.
Trap
Check interacting systemic drugs before prescribing.
Mioticcaution
Avoid
Susceptible peripheral retina, active inflammation, ciliochoroidal effusion mechanisms.
Expect
Brow ache, induced myopia, poor night vision.
Trap
Useless with an ischaemic sphincter, harmful in topiramate-type effusion.
Topical corticosteroidcaution
Avoid
Active HSV epithelial disease, fungal and Acanthamoeba keratitis, uncontrolled bacterial infection.
Monitor
IOP, epithelial healing, cataract, and the taper.
Trap
Name tissue, cause, dose, and monitoring, or the answer is incomplete.
